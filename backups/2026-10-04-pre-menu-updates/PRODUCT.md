# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static semantic HTML5, modern CSS, and minimal vanilla JavaScript. No framework, build tool, package manager, database, backend, or online checkout. Deploy directly to GitHub Pages and remain usable by opening `index.html` locally.

## Users

Primary users are mobile-first customers browsing handcrafted desserts for celebrations, thoughtful gifts, sharing, or personal treats. They need to understand the range quickly, compare sizes and prices, confirm availability expectations, and begin an enquiry through WhatsApp without signing in.

Bhoomika is the content maintainer. The implementation must remain approachable for a beginner updating products, prices, badges, contact details, and photographs.

## Product Purpose

Create a premium, emotionally warm, publicly shareable digital bakery menu for The Dessert Table by Bhoomika. Success means visitors recognize the brand quickly, browse products by category, scan a QR code to open the live menu, and enquire directly through WhatsApp.

## Positioning

The Dessert Table by Bhoomika is a boutique artisanal dessert studio centered on small-batch care, thoughtful gifting, homemade luxury, comfort, and meaningful celebrations rather than mass-market commerce.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive through a shared mobile link, browse without an account, navigate four menu categories, enquire through WhatsApp, share the menu, or print it. Real product photography is available for selected tea cakes and will be added incrementally, so the product must support a polished mix of real photographs and reference imagery without layout changes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Display exactly 17 supplied products from one JavaScript data source.
- Generate featured and category views from the same product data.
- Support missing-image fallbacks without broken-image icons or layout shift.
- Generate product-specific WhatsApp enquiry messages using a clearly marked number placeholder.
- Provide accessible mobile navigation, sticky category navigation, a scannable menu QR code, and direct WhatsApp enquiries.
- Include editable ordering, service-area, social, email, lead-time, delivery, pickup, custom-order, and payment placeholders.
- Include supplied allergen, alcohol, availability, and handmade-product disclosures without unconfirmed dietary claims.
- Preserve fast loading, keyboard support, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts from approximately 320px to 1440px, and GitHub Pages compatibility.
- Do not add a CRM, payment gateway, inventory system, admin portal, login, checkout, analytics, tracking, or fake form submission.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: The Dessert Table by Bhoomika
- Tagline: Crafted with Love. Shared with Joy. Remembered Forever.
- Voice: warm, sincere, calm, personal, refined, and never aggressively promotional.
- Confirmed palette starting points: cream white `#FFF9F3`, dusty rose `#C87E8A`, champagne gold `#D9B26F`, deep espresso `#3B2B24`, warm beige `#F3E7D9`, and white.
- The experience must communicate handcrafted quality, personal care, thoughtful gifting, homemade luxury, celebration, comfort, trust, and small-batch baking.
- Nanz Bakehouse, Maska Bakery, The Sugar Sifter, Pastry Kadai, and SAPA were supplied as high-level category references. Their proprietary designs, assets, and copy must not be reproduced.

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete product catalog with names, descriptions, sizes, prices, badge assignments, and planned image filenames is supplied in the implementation brief.
- Confirmed brand name, tagline, hero copy, founder note, ordering steps, and disclaimers are supplied.
- Real photography is available for selected tea cakes. A real logo, photography for the remaining products, location, fulfillment policy, and social accounts are not yet available and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Make personal care visible before asking for an order.
2. Keep browsing and enquiry effortless on a phone.
3. Treat the menu data as the single source of truth.
4. Preserve trust by distinguishing confirmed facts from editable placeholders.
5. Remain polished while real photography is introduced incrementally.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, a skip link, descriptive alternatives, sufficient contrast, visible focus, large tap targets, keyboard-operable navigation, reduced-motion support, accessible status messages, and no reliance on color alone. Maintain readable layouts from approximately 320px upward.
